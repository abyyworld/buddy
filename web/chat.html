Welcome to Buddy

Buddy thinks, talks and listens inside Tinfoil's sealed enclaves: nobody can read what you say, not even Tinfoil. It needs your own Tinfoil key:

1. Make an account at tinfoil.sh and create an API key (you pay Tinfoil for what you use).
2. Paste it here.

Kept only on this Mac, readable only by you. Never shown again or sent to your phone.
🔒

Pair with your Mac

On your Mac, open Buddy → Settings → Pair a phone, then type the 6-digit code here.

First add Buddy to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen), open it from there and pair in that app: it keeps its own pairing.

Pairing keeps everyone else out: other devices on your network, other people and other websites.
