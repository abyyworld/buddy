Less private: what changes

Normally Buddy's brain runs inside Tinfoil's sealed enclave: what you send is encrypted all the way into hardware nobody can look inside, not even Tinfoil, and Buddy checks that seal before it sends anything. That costs a little money.

The free brain runs on Cloudflare's ordinary servers (Workers AI, with your own free Cloudflare account). It's free up to a daily limit, but it's less private.

What goes to Cloudflare

  • What you type, and, with a Tinfoil key, what you say in voice messages and calls, as text (your voice itself is heard privately in Tinfoil first)
  • The conversation with that character since you turned Free on: nothing from before, and never a reply that drew on your notes or what they learned about you
  • Your characters' descriptions and the made-up lives Buddy gives them
  • What you write when you make a new character

What never goes to Cloudflare

  • Private chats: they stay in Tinfoil's enclave, or aren't sent at all without a Tinfoil key
  • Your notes, saved chats and what characters learned about you (so on Free they only remember the conversation)
  • Your photos (not even a description of them) and recordings, pictures, keys and backups
  • Companion chats, if you put Companion on Phala: they stay there, even if you switch that chat to Advisor
  • Whether you're married or single (where Buddy follows Islamic guidelines, Cloudflare only gets the rule that follows from it)

Without a Tinfoil key: typing only

Calls, talking, read-aloud, voice messages, photos and learning run only in Tinfoil's sealed enclave, never on the free brain. Without a Tinfoil key, Free is typing only.

Who can read it

Cloudflare can. Its servers see your messages in plain text while they answer. Whether it keeps them or uses them to train AI is up to its terms: today they say it does neither, but that's a promise Buddy can't check, and terms can change. A company that can read your data could also be hacked or made to hand it over.

What Tinfoil does instead

Tinfoil runs the AI in sealed hardware, and Buddy verifies the seal before connecting, so nobody, Tinfoil included, can read your chats. It's paid.

Free means limits

Cloudflare's free plan gives a daily allowance (roughly a couple of hundred replies, fewer in long chats). When it's used up, characters can't answer until it comes back at midnight UTC. Buddy tells you; it never switches to a paid brain on its own. Use an account on Cloudflare's free Workers plan: on Workers Paid, Cloudflare bills what goes over the allowance instead of stopping.

Switching back

Settings → Brain for everyday chats → Private, on any device. It applies from your next message; what was already sent stays under Cloudflare's terms. Private needs a Tinfoil key: without one, Buddy can't answer until you add it on your Mac (or turn Free back on).

Backup

Keep an encrypted copy of your conversations, characters, photos, settings and notes in a place you already trust: iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive or a USB drive. If this Mac is lost or breaks, you can bring everything back on a new one.

Only you can open it. It's locked with a recovery key that only you will have: not Buddy, not Apple, not Dropbox, not us. Lose the key and nobody can open your backups.
